everything about me

the long version.

hi

i'm madhup bhardwaj, a high school student in india who loves mathematics. i build small tools around how i learn, and share the ideas along the way.

still learning, always building.

right now

jee 2028 is the big one right now. building things is the passion that runs alongside it.

why math

it's the most logical thing there is. everything is rigorous, and if you're brave enough, you can derive it yourself. it doesn't always show up in real life, but i'm here for the love of the game.

what i build

i learn best by building. when a topic clicks, i want to make something out of it: a problem set, a whiteboard, a paper. most of it starts as a tool for myself, and some of it turns out to be useful for others too.
