hey,

i'm madhup, a high school student in india who loves mathematics. i build small tools around how i learn, and share the ideas along the way. still learning, always building.

i learn best by building.

when a topic clicks, i want to make something out of it: a problem set, a whiteboard, a paper. that way the idea has somewhere to live. most of what i make starts as a tool for myself, and some of it turns out to be useful for others too. if i had to pick a favourite, it's problemset.in, no contest. the rest live on the projects list.

jee 2028 is the big one right now. building things is the passion that runs alongside it.

people sometimes ask why math. it's the most logical thing there is. everything is rigorous, and if you're brave enough, you can derive it yourself. it doesn't always show up in real life, but that was never the point.

i'm here for the love of the game.

i'm not good at everything. art, for one, and i'll own it. drawing is not my strong suit.

on my shelf right now: calculus made easy by silvanus p. thompson, proofs by jay cummings, and the almanack of naval ravikant. that last one changed how i think. one of the most eye-opening books out there, and somehow still overlooked.

the easiest way to reach me is on linkedin. if you want to see how things are built, everything lives on github.

— madhup
